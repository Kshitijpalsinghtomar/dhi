## Release support policy

The official page states that each major Go release is supported until there are two newer major releases. Critical problems, including critical security problems, are fixed in supported releases through minor revisions.

Do not infer a security-support end date for Go 1.27 from this statement alone; the page describes the general support rule, not a published 1.27 end-of-support date.
